Work/Case study

Art Hawks.From brand to live marketplace.

Art Hawks turns cafés, bars, and neighbourhood rooms into a city-wide gallery. geodesAic took it from identity and architecture through to a live product with matching, payments, and venue operations.

Visit arthawks.comBook a similar build

The challenge

Monetise the room, skip the gallery.

Connect independent artists directly with commercial venues so physical rooms can sell original work — without gallery overhead, and without forcing either side onto a bloated template stack.

The solution

Map, QR, match, pay, collect.

A location-based platform: city map, room walls, QR on the hang, buy on the spot, collect in person. Automated venue–artist matching, a clean transaction pipeline, and onboarding that venues can actually run.

Work/Case study

EmailPOS.Enterprise email, actually production-ready.

EmailPOS is the campaign studio behind EPOS Audio. geodesAic built a full production pipeline — templates, editor, landing pages, QA, and analytics — so marketers can ship brand-safe email without living in Salesforce Marketing Cloud.

See a live landing pageBook a similar build

The challenge

Global brand. Painful production.

A global headset brand needed region- and channel-specific email and landing pages that matched eposaudio.com, passed production checks, and still sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Off-the-shelf ESPs could not own the EPOS template, the QA bar, or the channel split.

The solution

A studio that sits in front of Marketing Cloud.

A purpose-built studio: curated templates, a block editor, AI draft-from-URL, landing pages with the real EPOS header and footer, a production checker, asset library, and a dashboard over Marketing Cloud sends. Channel vs end-customer, region by region.

How it maps to geodesAic

One product, three capabilities.

Product enablement & web engineering

A real production app, not a plugin.

Email editor, landing-page builder, asset library, admin, and public LP rendering that wraps official EPOS chrome. Evidence geodesAic can ship an internal product teams actually open every week.

Business process & automation

Template, check, send, measure.

Generate-from-URL, shared templates with version history, a checker for size/links/unsubscribe, and Marketing Cloud analytics. The same AI-process story: remove handoffs between brand, regions, and the ESP.

Branding & go-to-market

EPOS identity, channel by channel.

Campaigns stay on-brand for partner and end-customer audiences. Landing pages look like eposaudio.com. The commercial system is the product: faster launches, fewer broken emails, one studio for a global team.

The tech stack

Purpose-built, not another ESP.

Custom PHP production app wired to Salesforce Marketing Cloud and GA4. Block editor, versioned templates, landing pages in EPOS chrome, and a checker that catches size, links, and unsubscribe issues before send.

The outcome

Live across regions and channels.

A live operations platform used for EPOS campaigns across UKI, DACH, France, Nordics and more. Marketers start from templates or a web page, pass the checker, publish a matching landing page, and read send performance without rebuilding the brand in an ESP.
